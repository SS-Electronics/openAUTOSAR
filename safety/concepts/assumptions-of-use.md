# Assumptions Of Use

Assumptions for the QEMU-first MVP:

- no physical ECU or vehicle actuation is controlled;
- virtual CAN and TAP interfaces are isolated test assets;
- applications are launched through the platform process launcher;
- service interfaces are generated from validated models;
- update packages include provenance and SBOM metadata;
- operators review release, safety, security, and legal evidence before delivery.
