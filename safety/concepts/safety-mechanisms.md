# Safety Mechanisms

Implemented or planned mechanisms:

- Platform Health Management supervision;
- State Management controlled transitions and degraded state;
- E2E protection for selected communication events;
- restart budgets and escalation;
- bounded queues and payload limits;
- safe hardware acceleration provider selection;
- UCM rollback on failed activation health checks;
- debug bundle collection for post-fault analysis.
