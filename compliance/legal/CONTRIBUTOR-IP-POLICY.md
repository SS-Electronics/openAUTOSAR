# Contributor IP Policy

Status: preliminary engineering control.

Contributions must be original work or compatible open-source work with clear
provenance.

## Contribution Rules

- Include a Developer Certificate of Origin sign-off.
- Do not submit copied AUTOSAR specification text or schemas.
- Do not submit downloaded specification PDFs.
- Do not submit proprietary ARXML, customer data, credentials, keys, or private
  certificates.
- Mark third-party source clearly and preserve upstream license notices.
- Keep generated files reproducible from controlled inputs.

## Review Rules

Maintainers must reject contributions that have unclear ownership,
incompatible licensing, confidential data, or unverifiable generation inputs.

## Security And Safety

Safety, security, runtime API, and release tooling changes require focused
review for ownership, traceability, and evidence impact.
